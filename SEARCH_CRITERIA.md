# Opportunity search criteria

## Goal

Find current, credible opportunities accessible from the UAE in applied AI, software, robotics, and cloud.

Prioritize opportunities that create real affiliation or backing: in-person UAE events, hackathons, selective programs, teams, mentors, research exposure, startup support, references, sponsored travel, internships, and communities where people can learn Yusuf's work.

A certificate alone is not a reason to recommend something.

## Check these sources first

1. 42 Abu Dhabi and verified partner opportunities
2. GDG Abu Dhabi and GDG Sharjah
3. UAE Robotics and Automation Society
4. MBZUAI public events and accessible programs
5. Hub71 programs and events
6. Dubai Future Foundation
7. Official UAE government, university, employer, incubator, and organizer sources
8. Devpost, Meetup, and Eventbrite

LinkedIn and social posts may reveal a lead, but the organizer or original registration page must confirm it. A completion or recap post is not an active opportunity.

## Classify first

Every incoming item must be classified before it is treated as an opportunity:

- `job`: paid employment
- `internship`: temporary work or practical placement
- `program`: fellowship, accelerator, incubator, mentorship, research, or selective cohort
- `event`: conference, meetup, workshop, talk, or networking event
- `course`: structured training; low priority unless it creates meaningful affiliation
- `competition`: challenge or contest
- `hackathon`: build-focused competition or team event
- `technical_news`: product, research, or industry update; not an opportunity
- `security_alert`: CVE, vulnerability, patch, or advisory; not an opportunity
- `completion_post`: a recap or someone's finished achievement; not a current opportunity
- `other`: unclear; do not recommend or save until manually classified

An AWS CVE bulletin is a `security_alert`, never a program or event.

## Required verification

Before recommending an item, record:

- Exact title and organizer
- Category
- Direct original source or official registration link
- Discovery source, if different
- Date checked
- Current listing status: `open`, `not_open_yet`, `closed`, `finished`, `cancelled`, or `unverified`
- Exact deadline and timezone, or `not stated`
- Event start and end dates
- Exact cost and currency, `free`, or `not stated`
- City, venue, and in-person, hybrid, or online format
- Eligibility
- Nationality or residency restrictions
- Student, age, employment, or experience restrictions
- Access: `public`, `invite_only`, `referral_required`, `eligibility_gated`, or `unclear`
- Concrete affiliation value
- Recommended next action

Never fill missing facts with guesses. If official sources conflict, use the newest official source and clearly note the conflict.

The Telegram bot does not browse or verify webpages. It stores only the text Yusuf supplies. When the labels above are included in pasted text, the MVP keeps the extra verification details in Notes.

## Recommendation rule

Recommend only when the item is verified, actionable, relevant, accessible from the UAE, and offers meaningful experience, affiliation, or backing.

Do not recommend closed or finished items, certificate-only courses, technical news, security alerts, completion posts, duplicates, or claims that cannot be traced to an original source.
